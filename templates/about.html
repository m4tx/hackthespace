about

Hack the Space is a cyber security CTF-like game. It consists of twelve puzzles spanning cryptanalysis, steganography, reverse engineering, and web application security. Board the pirate ship, put your skills to the test, and hack your way through space.

How to navigate

  • Begin at the start page.
  • Every puzzle, once solved, reveals the address of the next one. There is no menu — following the trail is part of the game.
  • Keep your eyes open — a couple of puzzles hide secret pages for the truly curious.
